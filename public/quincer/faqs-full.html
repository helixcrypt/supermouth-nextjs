Habits and Usage Questions

Should I brush before or after meals?

It's best to wait 30-60 minutes after eating before brushing to avoid damaging enamel softened by acids from food. To freshen your mouth and help neutralize acids immediately after eating, we recommend using our pH balancing mouth spray, which alkalizes saliva, provides prebiotics, and freshens your breath.

Is brushing enough, or do I need to floss as well?

Both brushing and flossing are essential for removing plaque and preventing cavities and gum disease. Additionally, using a tongue cleaner helps remove bacteria from the surface of your tongue, further improving oral hygiene and freshening breath.

Why should I use mouthwash?

Mouthwash helps reduce bacteria, freshen breath, and provide additional protection against tooth decay and gum disease. We recommend starting to use mouthwash at age 6 when children develop their swallowing reflexes. It can also reach areas in the mouth that brushing and flossing might miss.

How often should I have a dental exam and cleaning?

Visit your dentist every six months for a thorough exam and cleaning to maintain optimal oral health.

What's the best oral care routine for me or my child?

An effective oral care routine includes brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste and/or Hydroxamin-based toothpaste, flossing daily, using mouthwash and mouth spray, and visiting your dentist regularly. Incorporate the use of a tongue cleaner to remove bacteria from the tongue's surface. Taking our oral care assessment can help determine the best products for your family's needs.

When is the best time to brush/floss each day?

Brush in the morning before breakfast to remove plaque and bacteria that accumulate overnight and before bed to clean your teeth and gums after the day's meals. Floss once a day, preferably before brushing at night. Use a tongue cleaner as part of your nightly routine.

How often should I change my toothbrush?

Replace your toothbrush every three months or sooner if the bristles are frayed. Softer, higher-quality bristles may require more frequent replacement, approximately every 6 weeks. Also, replace it after recovering from an illness.

How long should I wait after eating to brush?

Wait at least 30-60 minutes after eating to brush to protect your enamel from acid erosion. In the meantime, you can use our pH balancing mouth spray to freshen your mouth and help neutralize acids.

Is sugar the only cause of cavities?

No, while sugar is a major factor, poor oral hygiene, acidic foods and drinks, and dry mouth also contribute to cavity formation.

What causes cavities?

Cavities are caused by the buildup of plaque and bacteria on teeth, which produce acids that erode enamel. Contributing factors include poor oral hygiene, sugary diets, acidic foods and drinks, dry mouth, and infrequent dental visits.

What causes bad breath, and how can I improve it?

Bad breath is often caused by bacteria in the mouth, poor oral hygiene, certain foods, and medical conditions. Improve it by brushing and flossing regularly, using mouthwash, cleaning your tongue, and staying hydrated. Regular dental check-ups can also help identify and address underlying issues.

How can I choose the right toothpaste for my family?

Consider factors such as fluoride content, sensitivity, whitening, and whether a Hydroxamin-based product is suitable. Our oral care assessment can help you select the best products tailored to your family's needs.

What should I look for on oral care product labels?

Look for key active ingredients such as fluoride or nano-hydroxyapatite, which provide enamel protection and repair. Avoid products with artificial colors, flavors, sweeteners, and preservatives. Ensure the product is free of SLS, GMOs, antibiotics, and "forever chemicals."
