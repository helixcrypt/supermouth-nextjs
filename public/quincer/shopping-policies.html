SuperMouth shopping policies: systems, subscription, shipping, guarantee, SuperBucks and referrals

Customer-facing policies as published on supermouth.com: system supply and savings, subscription discount, cadence and cancellation, free shipping, the 30-day guarantee, SuperBucks rewards and the referral program.

Systems

Supply period

Every System is a full 90-day supply (3 months).

Bundle savings

Save 15% or more on all systems vs. buying items separately.

Subscription

Subscription discount

10% more off when you subscribe (on top of system savings); site also says "save up to 25% with subscription". Example: Ortho Toothpaste Bundle $57.59 on subscription vs $63.99.

Cadence

Fresh box every 90 days; monthly or quarterly payment options; delivery schedule adjustable.

Cancellation

Cancel any time; if cancelled after a shipment, billed for the rest of the 90-day cycle.

Shipping

Free shipping

Free shipping on orders over $50 or on any Complete system.

Guarantee

30-day guarantee

Whole-Mouth Satisfaction Guaranteed: 30-day money-back guarantee (system and product pages). FAQ: 30-day return policy on "most products".

SuperBucks

Earning

1 SB per $1 (product subtotal; excludes tax, shipping, gift cards); +100 SB on joining; 1.25x SB on subscription renewals; +50 SB verified review; +100 SB photo/video review; +50 SB assessment (once); +100 SB birthday.

Redemption levels

100 SB = $5 off or free item up to $9.99; 500 SB = $25 off or up to $49.99; 750 SB = $50 off or up to $74.99; 1,000 SB = $75 off or up to $99.99; 2,500 SB = $200 off or up to $250.

Expiry

SB expire after 18 months of inactivity; purchase SB post after the order ships.

Referral

Consumer referral

Friend gets $10 off first order of $40+; referrer earns 200 SB when it ships; milestones +300 SB (3), +300 SB (5), +900 SB (10). First-time customers only.

Professional

SuperMouth Pro

Dentists, physicians and hygienists can join SuperMouth Pro. No discount amount or D2C attribution rule is published.
